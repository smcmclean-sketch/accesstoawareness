How to join the Live Call, step by step

This guide takes you through everything slowly, one tap at a time. You only need to do it once. After that, the call is waiting for you every Wednesday.

Before you begin, have these ready:
  • Your phone (or a computer)
  • Your email address, and access to your email inbox
  • A password you will remember. Write it down somewhere safe.
1

Open the website

  1. On your phone, open the internet app.
    On an iPhone, this is Safari (the blue compass).
    On an Android phone, this is usually Chrome (the red, yellow and green circle).
  2. Tap the long bar at the top or bottom of the screen, where it says Search or enter website name.
  3. Type: sophiemclean.com
  4. Tap Go on the keyboard. Sophie's website opens.
On a small screen the menu may be hidden. Look for three short lines (☰) in a top corner and tap them to see the menu.
2

Register (create your account)

  1. Open the menu (☰) and tap Register. It may also be called Sign up or Log in. If you only see Log in, tap it, then tap Register on the next page.
  2. Fill in the boxes. Tap each box once and type:
    • your name
    • your email address
    • a password (and, if asked, the same password again)
  3. If there is a small box to tick (for example, to agree to the terms), tap it so a tick appears.
  4. Tap the Register button at the bottom.
  5. Now open your email. Look for a message from Sophie McLean's website. It usually arrives within a few minutes.
  6. Open that email and tap the link inside it. This confirms your account. You may be asked to choose your password here.
No email after 10 minutes? Look in your Spam or Junk folder, and on Gmail also the Promotions tab.
3

Join the Sophie McLean Live call

  1. Go back to sophiemclean.com and make sure you are logged in. If you are not, tap Log in and type your email and password.
  2. Open the menu (☰) and tap Live (the Live Call page).
  3. Scroll down to How to join and tap Join the live call.
  4. On the next page, tap Join or Enrol. You are now in the Sophie McLean Live group. It is free.
  5. That's it. Each week, the link to the call is waiting for you in this group. Just before the call, come back here and tap the link to join.

4

Put the icon on your phone

This puts a little Sophie McLean button on your phone's home screen, like an app. One tap, and you are back at the call. First, open the Live Call group page from Step 3, so the icon takes you straight there.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen (on some iPhones, tap ••• first).
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. You can change the name, for example to Sophie Live.
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).
  3. You can change the name, for example to Sophie Live.
  4. Tap Add, then Add again if asked.

Now close the internet app and look at your home screen. The new icon is there. Tap it any time to open the Live Call.

If something goes wrong

I forgot my password.
On the Log in page, tap Lost your password? (or Forgot password). Type your email, and a link to make a new password is sent to you.
It says my email is already registered.
You already have an account. Tap Log in instead, and use "Lost your password?" if you need to.
I can't find the icon on my phone.
Swipe left across your home screens; it may be on the last page. On Android, it can also be in the list of all apps.
I'm still stuck.
Write to Sophie through the Contact page, and say which step you are on.

Come as you are. There is nothing to prepare, and nothing to prove.
